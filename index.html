Tam Truong

PhD Candidate in Bioinformatics & Algorithms · GenScale Team, IRISA / Inria

I am a doctoral researcher in the GenScale team at IRISA / Inria (Rennes, France), advised by Karel Břinda, Dominique Lavenier, and Pierre Peterlongo. My research focuses on scalable algorithms and storage for million-genome bacterial collections—leveraging evolutionary relationships to reorder genomes, reduce entropy, and enable efficient compression, indexing, and random access.

During my MSc, I worked on discrete optimization for long-read metagenomic strain separation.
